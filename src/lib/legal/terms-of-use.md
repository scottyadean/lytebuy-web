## 5. Fees

Lytebuy may charge:

- An **in-person (POS) commission** on Tap-to-Pay charges.

Current fee rates are disclosed in the app and may change with notice. Payment
processing fees charged by our payment provider are borne by the Vendor. **Sales tax
